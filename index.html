The Home

Three floors, built for treetop living

Three bedrooms, each with its own private bathroom, are spread across three light-filled floors — every one of them, plus the living room, cooled by its own A/C unit. The house came fully furnished in 2018 and has been kept that way since: nothing to buy, nothing to wait on.

High-speed Wi-Fi covers every floor, and the layout is as easy for a family of six as it is for a couple who just wants the primary suite to themselves.

Kitchen & Living

Cook, gather, unwind

The kitchen has everything a home cook — or a private chef, if you'd rather book one — needs to work with: full-size refrigerator, oven, cooktop, and small appliances down to the coffee maker. A granite island seats six for breakfast before anyone's made it to the beach.

In the living room, a deep sectional faces a flat-screen wired for Apple TV, a Bluetooth speaker keeps the volume going, and a powder room means guests never have to climb a flight mid-party. Slide the glass doors open and the jungle-style back patio — shaded by bamboo — becomes part of the room.

Bedrooms & Baths

Room for family, room to spread out

All three bedrooms are ensuite. Every bed is dressed and ready — no move-in shopping list required.

Community & Location

Aldea Zama, from the inside

Casa Jade sits inside a gated community built around two swimming pools, a sundeck, a thatched-roof palapa, a grill, and security on duty around the clock — all shared space, all a short walk from your own front door.

  • 2 outdoor pools
  • Sundeck & showers
  • Palapa & BBQ grill
  • 24-hour security
  • Covered parking
  • Beach club access

Ask and the current owners will arrange a private chef, a massage therapist, guided tours, cenote trips, or a table at their favorite restaurant. Aldea Zama itself is one of Tulum's fastest-growing neighborhoods — safe and walkable, filling in with new restaurants, coffee shops and boutiques every season, and close to the beach, the town, and the Mayan ruins outside it. Most owners rent a car; bicycles, taxis and buses cover the rest.
